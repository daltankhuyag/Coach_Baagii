Program Overview

A Serious Room Built For Young Wrestlers

The Youth Club is not a babysitting service with mats. It is a structured program where younger athletes learn how to train, how to compete, and how to hold themselves to a standard from the very first day.

Foundation

What We Build

  • Athletic stance and proper motion
  • Hand fighting and collar ties
  • Level change and penetration step
  • Top and bottom position fundamentals
  • Breakdowns and short offense from top
  • Stand-up and hip movement from bottom
Standards

What We Expect

  • On time and ready to work
  • Effort on every single rep
  • Respect for training partners
  • Coachability above everything
  • Eyes on the coach during instruction
  • Compete hard, win clean, lose with pride
Results

What Athletes Gain

  • Confidence in competitive situations
  • A technical base that carries to higher levels
  • Stronger body awareness and athleticism
  • Discipline that transfers outside the room
  • A clear understanding of how to train
  • Real match experience in a safe setting

What A Practice Looks Like

How We Run A Session

Typical 90-Minute Practice

Every session follows the same structure so athletes know what to expect and can measure their own progress over time.

01
Warm-Up & Movement (15 min)

Dynamic stretching, wrestling-specific agility work, and body positioning drills. Builds athleticism while prepping joints for live work.

02
Technique Block (30 min)

Coach Baagii introduces or refines one to two positions. Full demonstration, coached repetitions, and partner drilling with individual correction.

03
Situational Drilling (20 min)

Athletes apply the technique from a specific starting position — standing, on top, or on bottom. Controlled intensity with clear objectives.

04
Live Wrestling (15 min)

Supervised live goes with age and skill-matched partners. Emphasis on using what was covered in practice, not just surviving.

05
Cool-Down & Recap (10 min)

Stretching, quick review of the session's focal points, and individual feedback where needed before athletes leave the mat.

Common Questions

What Parents Ask

Does my child need prior wrestling experience?

No. The Youth Club accepts beginners. We build from stance and motion regardless of background, so athletes who have never wrestled before will be coached at their level from the first session.

What age range does the Youth Club cover?

Ages 6 through 13. If your child is close to the edge of that range and you are unsure of the right fit, come to a tryout session and Coach Baagii will make a recommendation based on what he sees in the room.

Is this the same as a school wrestling team?

No. The Youth Club is a club program with smaller groups, more individual coaching attention, and a focus on technical development rather than meeting school eligibility requirements. Many athletes do both simultaneously.

How physical is it? Should I be concerned about injuries?

Contact is controlled and age-appropriate at this level. Live drilling and live wrestling are supervised closely. Coach Baagii prioritizes technical development over uncontrolled roughhousing.

Can my child compete in tournaments through this program?

Yes. Coach Baagii will identify tournament opportunities appropriate for each athlete's development stage and let parents know when competition makes sense.
