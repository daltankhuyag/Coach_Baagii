Program Overview

Three Ways To Train

Each track is built around the same values: strength, toughness, discipline, and resilience. The difference is the level of detail, pace, and individual attention each athlete needs.

Youth Club

For younger wrestlers who need fundamentals, confidence, and room structure that keeps standards high without overwhelming them.

Scholastic Squad

For middle and high school wrestlers chasing competitive growth, sharper match tactics, and better live performance.

Private Sessions

For focused technical correction, individual game plans, and faster improvement in specific positions or sequences.

Training Paths

Learn More About Each Program

01 · Youth Foundation

Youth Club

Ages 6-13. This program introduces stance, motion, basic attacks, mat awareness, and the core folkstyle positions in a room designed to build confidence and coachable habits from the start.

Best For

Newer wrestlers and younger athletes building fundamentals.

Training Focus

Stance, motion, hand fighting, level changes, and top-bottom basics.

Schedule

Tue and Thu, 6-7:30 PM. Structured reps with positive accountability.

02 · Competitive Room

Scholastic Squad

Built for middle and high school wrestlers who need more live work, sharper chain wrestling, and better tactical decisions against real pressure. This room is designed for athletes pushing toward postseason results.

Best For

Experienced youth, middle school, and high school wrestlers.

Training Focus

Situational wrestling, live goes, pace management, and match strategy.

Schedule

Mon, Wed, Fri, 5-7 PM with a strong emphasis on competition transfer.

03 · Focused Correction

Private Sessions

Private work is for wrestlers who need a specific problem solved fast, whether that means leg attacks, mat returns, top pressure, hand fighting sequences, or a custom plan leading into a tournament.

Best For

Athletes targeting a weakness or preparing for a key stretch of the season.

Training Focus

Individual technique, film-informed adjustments, and repeatable scoring sequences.

Schedule

By appointment for one-on-one or small-group technical work.
