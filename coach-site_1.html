The Coach

Baterdene "Baagii" Boldmaa

Originally from Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, I built one of the most dominant careers in NAIA history at Doane University — three national titles at 141 pounds, a 116–3 overall record, and a 100-match winning streak that ran undefeated for more than three years.

Before Doane, I wrestled at Northwest Kansas Technical College, where I was a two-time NJCAA national champion. Across both programs I earned five collegiate All-American honors. In March 2025, I was inducted into the Doane Athletics Hall of Fame.

I coach the way I trained: fundamentals first, relentless position, and the kind of conditioning that wins the third period. Whether you're a first-year youth wrestler or a varsity starter chasing a state berth, my job is to make the hard work pay off — and to keep the room demanding but positive.
