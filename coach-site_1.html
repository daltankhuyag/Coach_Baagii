The Coach

Baagii Built The Edge. The Brand Now Shows It.

Originally from Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, Baterdene "Baagii" Boldmaa built one of the most dominant runs in NAIA wrestling history at Doane University: three national titles at 141 pounds, a 116-3 record, and a win streak that reached 100 matches.

Before Doane, he won two NJCAA national titles at Northwest Kansas Technical College. Across both levels he earned five collegiate All-American honors and joined the Doane Athletics Hall of Fame in March 2025.

The coaching style matches the logo energy: pressure, discipline, and intent. Fundamentals stay sharp, conditioning stays demanding, and every athlete is expected to wrestle with control late in the match.

Fundamentals first. No wasted motion, no soft positions.
Pressure with purpose. Hard work has to become technical work.
Positive room, high standards. The tone stays demanding without losing discipline.
